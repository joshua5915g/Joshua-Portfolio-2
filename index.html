    I’m a computer science student who builds things to understand them. I run a digital services business, I’ve shipped five live apps, and I care as much about how software feels as how it works.

    Hello, I’m Joshua.

    I’m 20, from Kerala, and I finished my Higher Secondary in Computer Science at St Mary’s Cathedral Public School. For the last two years I’ve run a digital communications business that handles e-services, which taught me why secure, well-architected systems matter in the real world.
